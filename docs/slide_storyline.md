# Slide Storyline

## Slide 1 - Case Question

Should FlowFinance expand from SMB analytics into embedded finance?

## Slide 2 - Recommendation

Enter through credit-readiness analytics, not direct lending.

## Slide 3 - Market Size

TAM/SAM/SOM with explicit digitally reachable and serviceable filters.

## Slide 4 - Customer Segmentation

Prioritize digitally active MSMEs with recurring transactions and willingness to pay for cashflow clarity.

## Slide 5 - Competitive Landscape

Position between accounting software, payment aggregators, banks, and lenders.

## Slide 6 - Unit Economics

Pro tier has the best wedge economics: Rs 799/month, 82% gross margin, Rs 14,414 LTV, 2.14-month payback.

## Slide 7 - GTM Roadmap

Accountants/CA firms first, fintech customers second, lender partnerships after data-quality validation.

## Slide 8 - Risks and Decision

Proceed with analytics-first GTM; avoid direct lending and regulatory overreach.
