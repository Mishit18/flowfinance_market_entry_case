# Case Interview Defense

## 30-Second Pitch

I evaluated whether FlowFinance should expand from MSME analytics into embedded finance. The recommendation is to enter through credit-readiness analytics, not direct lending, because it uses existing transaction categorization and health-score capabilities while avoiding balance-sheet, regulatory, and collections risk.

## Why The Recommendation Is Not Direct Lending

- Direct lending requires capital, collections, underwriting governance, and regulatory controls.
- FlowFinance already has an analytics wedge: transaction categorization, cashflow health, budgeting, and lender-readiness reports.
- Partnering with lenders lets FlowFinance monetize insight without taking credit risk.

## Key Numbers

| Metric | Value |
|---|---:|
| Pro tier price | Rs. 799/month |
| Pro tier gross margin | 82% |

| Pro tier CAC payback | 2.14 months |
| Year-1 SOM | 2,500 customers |
| Year-1 revenue potential | Rs. 18M |

## Sensitivity Defense

The case still works in the moderate downside scenarios because payback stays below 3 months under ARPA down 25% or CAC up 25%. In the bear case, the recommendation shifts to partner-led acquisition only because paid CAC becomes too risky.

## Risks

- Low willingness-to-pay among micro merchants
- Category accuracy and data-quality trust
- Regulatory perception if marketed as credit scoring
- Slow lender partnership cycles

## What I Would Track Weekly

- Activation rate
- Categorization correction rate
- Weekly active businesses
- Budget alert engagement
- Gross margin
- CAC by channel
- 30/60/90-day retention
- Lender-readiness report exports
